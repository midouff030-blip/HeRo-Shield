FAQ

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. HeRo Shield is 100% free for every server, no player limit.

Does it work with my framework?

Yes: ESX, QBCore, Qbox or standalone. It detects your framework by itself. OneSync is required.

Can admins get banned?

No. Admins are always ignored. Tip: set detections to "log" for 1-2 days, then switch to "ban".

How do I unban someone?

From the dashboard (Bans tab) or in console with hsunban HS-XXXXXX.

Does it cause lag?

No. Checks are light and run once per second.
